I'm a Computer Science & Engineering undergraduate at BRAC University, focused on turning complex AI questions into measurable experiments and useful systems.

My current research direction is asynchronous pretraining and model merging for large language models. I'm particularly interested in whether independently trained model components can be combined efficiently while retaining useful downstream performance.

Beyond research, I work on NLP experimentation, data-driven projects, databases, and full-stack applications. I enjoy collaborating across research and product-oriented teams.

ONGOING RESEARCH DIRECTION R/01

Asynchronous Pretraining & Model Merging for LLMs

Studying how independently pretrained model shards can be combined into a larger model, and how resulting quality compares with conventionally trained baselines. The work examines practical efficiency alongside downstream language performance.
